Welcome to The Loom

A programme raises average income. But how many people actually benefit?

Try different ways of matching incomes with and without the programme. Find the smallest and largest percentages who could benefit.

How to play

What you see. The left column shows incomes without the programme; the right shows incomes with it. Each circle represents the same share of the population. We know both lists exactly, but not which incomes belong to the same people.

What you can change. Each line pairs two incomes for the same people. Click two circles on the right to swap their connections, or drag one onto another. You can try any matching that uses each circle once and follows the rules shown above the diagram. You cannot change the incomes.

What the lines mean. A line from 1 to 3 means an income gain of 2. Green means a gain, red a loss, and dashed grey no change. These are possible matches, not known facts about individuals.

Your task. Find the lowest percentage that answers the question and click “Save minimum”. Then find the highest and click “Save maximum”. Click “Check my answer” when ready. Your saved answers stay in place as you experiment. If the minimum and maximum are equal, the information gives one exact answer.

Keyboard: Tab to a right-hand circle, then Enter or Space to select it. Repeat on another circle to swap connections.
